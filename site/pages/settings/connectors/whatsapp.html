    WhatsApp¶

    Send your campaign messages on WhatsApp, from your own WhatsApp Business number.

    What it's for¶

    With the WhatsApp connector, GoodBards sends WhatsApp messages to the contacts who agreed to receive them, from your number and under your business name. Everything goes through your own Meta account: Meta bills you directly for the messages, and GoodBards never pays or sees your Meta bill.

    In development

    Today you can connect your WhatsApp number and check its status. Message templates and sending to your contacts arrive next. Meta's screens are made for developers: if you would rather not do this alone, contact support and we set it up with you on a short call.

    Before you start¶

    • WhatsApp is included in the Pro and Full plans.
    • You must be an administrator or the owner of your GoodBards company account.
    • You need a Facebook account that is an administrator of your company's Meta business portfolio (business.facebook.com). Ideally the portfolio is already verified by Meta.
    • Allow 20 to 30 minutes. You do this once.

    The GoodBards form, field by field¶

    At the end, GoodBards asks for four details. Here is what each one is, in plain words, and where it comes from. The steps below collect them one by one.

    Field in GoodBards What it is Where Meta shows it Looks like
    WhatsApp Business Account ID Your WhatsApp account at Meta, which holds your number(s) and message templates. Meta now calls it Messaging account ID. Your app's WhatsApp setup page (step 2), or Business settings → Messaging accounts → your account → ID 15–16 digits, e.g. 102290129340398
    Phone number ID Meta's internal number for your sending phone line. Not the phone number itself. Your app's WhatsApp setup page, next to the number (step 2) 15–16 digits, e.g. 106540352242922
    Permanent access token The key that lets GoodBards send in your name. Shown only once. Business settings → System users → Generate token (step 4) A long text starting with EAA
    App secret Proves that messages and updates really come from your Meta app. Your app → App settings → Basic → App secret → Show (step 3) 32 letters and digits

    Keep a note open

    Paste each detail in a private note as you go (your password manager is ideal), then copy them into GoodBards at the end.

    How to set up WhatsApp in Meta¶

    1. Create a Meta app¶

    The app is your company's "mailbox" at Meta for sending WhatsApp messages.

    1. Go to developers.facebook.com, log in, and choose My Apps → Create App.

    2. Give the app a name, for example Your brand Messaging. Meta refuses names that contain "WhatsApp".

    3. Choose Next, then tick Connect with customers through WhatsApp.

    4. Choose Next and pick your company's business portfolio.

    5. Choose Next twice. The Requirements step lists Business verification and App Review: App Review is only for apps that access other businesses' accounts, so it does not stop you from sending from your own number.

    6. Check the summary and choose Create app. This accepts Meta's Platform Terms for your business.

    2. Copy the Phone number ID and the WhatsApp Business Account ID¶

    1. On the app's dashboard, choose Customize the Connect with customers through WhatsApp use case.

    2. Meta shows a guided setup. Choose Step 1. Try it out.

    3. Meta gives you a free test number. Next to it:

      • Phone Number ID → paste it in GoodBards' Phone number ID.
      • Messaging account ID → paste it in GoodBards' WhatsApp Business Account ID.

    The test number is enough to try GoodBards. To send from your real number, follow Step 2. Production setup on the same page: add your number, then a payment method. Your real number then has its own Phone number ID, shown in the same place.

    3. Copy the app secret¶

    1. In the app, open App settings → Basic (left menu, near the bottom).
    2. Next to App secret, choose Show. Meta asks for your Facebook password.
    3. Copy the secret → GoodBards' App secret.

    4. Create the permanent access token¶

    GoodBards needs a key that does not expire. Meta gives such keys to a system user: a robot account of your company that can only do what you allow. You create it, give it access to your app and to your WhatsApp account, then generate its key.

    a. Create the system user

    1. Go to business.facebook.com → Settings → Users → System users and choose Add. The first time, Meta asks you to accept its non-discrimination policy.

    2. Name it, for example GoodBards Sender, keep the role Employee, and choose Create system user.

    3. With the system user selected, choose Assign assets → Apps, tick your new app, turn on Develop app, and choose Assign assets.

    b. Give it access to your WhatsApp account (the step people miss)

    1. Still in Settings, open Accounts → Messaging accounts and select your WhatsApp account.

    2. Choose Assign people, tick your system user, turn on Everything (full access), and choose Assign.

    3. On the account's People tab, check that your system user is listed under People with full access.

    Use Messaging accounts, not WhatsApp accounts

    Meta also lists your account under Assign assets → WhatsApp accounts on the system user page. Giving access there is not enough: GoodBards then shows "Meta cannot find this WhatsApp Business Account". Always check the People tab of the messaging account.

    c. Generate the key

    1. Back in System users, select your system user and choose Generate token.

    2. Pick your app.

    3. Set the expiry to Never. With 60 days, your WhatsApp sending would stop until you create a new token.

    4. Tick whatsapp_business_management and whatsapp_business_messaging, then choose Generate token.

    5. Copy the token right away → GoodBards' Permanent access token. Meta shows it only once; if you lose it, generate a new one.

    Keep the token secret

    Anyone with this token can send WhatsApp messages as your business. Paste it only in GoodBards, never in an email or a chat.

    How to connect in GoodBards¶

    1. Open your account menu (your picture, top right), then choose Connections.

    2. On the Messaging tab, find WhatsApp and choose Connect.

    3. Paste the four details (see the table above) and choose Connect WhatsApp.

    GoodBards checks everything with Meta before saving. If Meta refuses, nothing is saved and the message says which detail is wrong: see If it does not work below. Once connected, the card shows your business name, your number and your WhatsApp profile picture (or your initials when you have none); Number & webhook shows Meta's quality rating.

    How to receive delivery updates and replies¶

    Meta tells GoodBards when a message is delivered or read, and when someone replies or asks to stop, through a webhook. You set it once:

    1. In GoodBards, on the WhatsApp card, choose Number & webhook and copy the Callback URL and Verify token.
    2. In your Meta app, open WhatsApp → Configuration → Webhook → Edit, paste both, and choose Verify and save.
    3. Under Webhook fields, subscribe to messages and message_template_status_update.

    If it does not work¶

    GoodBards shows Meta's refusal in plain words, with Meta's own text in brackets.

    GoodBards says Why What to do
    Meta cannot find this WhatsApp Business Account or phone number with this token. Most often the system user has no access to the account… (Meta: Object with ID … does not exist, cannot be loaded due to missing permissions) Most often: the system user has no access to the WhatsApp account. Or an ID was mistyped. Do step 4b: Messaging accounts → your account → People → your system user with Everything. Check both IDs. Then choose Connect WhatsApp again; the same token works.
    The access token is not valid or has expired The token was copied incompletely, revoked, or set to expire. Generate a new token (step 4c) with expiry Never, and paste it again.
    The app secret does not belong to the app that created this token The secret comes from another app, or the token was generated for another app. Copy the secret from the app you picked in step 4c (App settings → Basic).
    The token is missing a permission One of the two permissions was not ticked. Generate a new token with whatsapp_business_management and whatsapp_business_messaging.
    The phone number ID is a number… The phone number itself was pasted. Paste the Phone Number ID shown next to the number (step 2).
    This phone number ID is not part of this WhatsApp Business Account The two IDs come from different accounts. Copy both from the same place (step 2).

    Good to know¶

    • Meta bills you, per marketing message, at Meta's rate for the recipient's country. Add a payment method in WhatsApp Manager before sending from your real number.
    • The test number can only message a few phone numbers that you add in Meta (up to 5). Use it to try things, not for campaigns.
    • Quality rating. Meta rates your number from how people react to your messages. A low rating lowers how many people you can message per day: only message contacts who agreed.
    • Consent. GoodBards only sends to contacts with WhatsApp consent, and a "STOP" reply withdraws it.
    • To use a new token or number, choose Connect again with the new details: your webhook keeps working.

    How to disconnect¶

    1. On the WhatsApp card, choose Disconnect.
    2. GoodBards forgets the token and app secret. Nothing changes in your Meta account; to stop the key completely, remove the system user's token in Business settings (Revoke tokens).

    Still stuck? Contact support.
