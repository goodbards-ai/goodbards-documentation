    Organic Visibility¶

    How your website is found, in search engines and in AI assistants, and what to fix first.

    Who sees it: everyone your administrator gives the Website and SEO access to.

    Before you start¶

    These screens read your Google Search Console and Google Analytics data. If a screen says you are not connected, start at Connections.

    The screens¶

    Performance & Report¶

    How your site performs in search over time: clicks, impressions and position, with the trend and what to expect next. Other tabs give an executive report to share and a health check of your site.

    Page Fixes¶

    The pages worth fixing first: pages close to the top of the results, pages losing traffic, and pages people see but rarely click. Each card says what to change, and you can send it to Actions.

    AI Readiness & Authority¶

    Whether AI assistants can read, trust and quote your pages, scored page by page, and how often they mention you when asked about your market.
