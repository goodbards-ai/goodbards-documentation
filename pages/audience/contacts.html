    Contacts¶

    The people you market to — how to add them, keep their details current, and group them into segments.

    What it's for¶

    Every email you send and every form someone fills in comes back to a contact. Contacts are the centre of the platform: get them right and the rest of your marketing has something to work with.

    Adding contacts¶

    There is more than one way, depending on how many you have:

    Method Use it when
    Add one at a time You met someone and want them in straight away.
    Import You have a list to bring across. Progress shows under Import Jobs.
    Card Scanner You came back from an event with a stack of business cards. Scan them and the details are read for you.
    Forms Someone signs up themselves. See Forms.

    Reading the Contacts home¶

    Audience → Contacts opens on the Home tab, which summarises your database rather than listing individuals.

    The tiles across the top tell you:

    • Total Contacts — how many you hold, and how many your plan allows. The bar fills as you approach the limit.
    • Subscribed — contacts who have agreed to hear from you.
    • Regular — contacts with no marketing consent yet: you can work with them, but not email them marketing.
    • Others (unsubscribed, do not contact) — people you must not email.

    Subscribed is the number that matters

    A large database is not much use if most of it has unsubscribed. Only Subscribed contacts can receive marketing email.

    Score Progression below tracks your contacts' average scores over time; it builds up day by day.

    The grades¶

    Contact Readiness grades how usable your database is, and the engagement grade how much your contacts interact with you. Each is a score out of 100 shown as a letter: A (90–100), B (75–89), C (60–74), D (40–59) or F (below 40). Choose the grade to see its formula.

    Grade Made of
    Readiness Profile completeness (50 points): how many contacts have a first and last name, company, job title, country, preferred language and phone. Volume (25 points): how many contacts you hold. Growth momentum (25 points): new contacts this month against last month.
    Engagement Engaged share (50 points): contacts with a score of 40 or more. Reach (25 points): contacts with any engagement at all. Depth (25 points): the average score per contact.

    To raise readiness, fill in the missing details (an import with the Overwrite conflict action can complete existing contacts). To raise engagement, send your contacts something worth opening. See Lead scoring.

    Companies have the same two grades, built from company data. See Companies.

    Finding individual contacts¶

    The Details List tab lists every contact: their code, name, owner, email, status and last update.

    • Search by name or email, and choose the columns to show with View.
    • Each contact can have owners, the colleagues responsible for them. Filter by owner, or select contacts and choose Assign Owners. Depending on their role, a colleague may only see the contacts they own.
    • Export As CSV downloads the list; Add Contact creates one.
    • Click a contact's code to open their record: details, custom fields, mailing lists, and the history of every email, form and event.

    A contact's page¶

    Choose a contact's code in the list to open their page: everything GoodBards knows about them, in one place.

    Section What it shows
    Scores The engagement score and the decay score next to the name. See Lead scoring.
    Contact Summary Job, companies, email, phone, customer type, preferred contact method, referral source and owners.
    Location Country, city, address and timezone.
    Custom Fields Your own fields, such as a loyalty tier or a home store.
    Policies The privacy statements the contact accepted, when, and how (for example through a form). This is your proof of consent.
    Mailing Lists The lists they belong to, how they joined (public sign-up, added manually) and whether they are still subscribed.
    Events and Form submissions The events they registered for and the forms they filled in.
    Social Links, Referral Info, Tags Their social profiles, where they came from, and your tags.
    Engagement Over Time and Engagement Timeline Every open, click, form, registration and subscription, day by day and in order.

    Editing a contact¶

    Choose Edit Contact at the top right. The form has the personal details, address and timezone, job and companies, subscription status, social handles, referral source, preferred contact method, customer type, privacy statement, mailing lists, tags and Contact Notes. Choose Save Contact when done.

    • Notes keep what your team knows that fits no field: met at the Mid Valley styling clinic. Choose Add Note.
    • Adding a mailing list here subscribes the contact straight away. Only do it for people who agreed.
    • Owners can be changed here by an administrator. See Users, roles & permissions.

    Segments¶

    A segment is a saved filter — a rule that decides who belongs, evaluated when you use it, so it stays current as contacts change.

    To create one, open the Segments tab and choose Add Segment, then set the rule. A segment can filter on contact details (such as country or job title), on behaviour (what contacts did) and on alerts.

    Choose a segment's name to open it: its description, its filters, a summary of which kinds of filter are applied, and the list of contacts it holds right now. Edit Segment changes the rule.

    Useful segments look like:

    • Singapore members — everyone in one market
    • Corporate buyers — one type of customer
    • Bangkok launch list — the people from one event

    Segments beat one-off filters

    If you find yourself applying the same filter repeatedly, save it as a segment. Give it a description too — in six months you will not remember what the rule was.

    Mailing Lists¶

    Lists people subscribe to, for example from a sign-up form. See Mailing lists.

    Card Scanner¶

    Photograph a business card, or upload a picture of one: GoodBards reads it and fills in a new contact for you to check and save. See Adding contacts.

    Custom Fields¶

    The details GoodBards does not hold by default, but your business needs: a loyalty tier, a preferred store, a renewal date.

    1. Open the Custom Fields tab, then create a field.

    2. Give it a Field Name and a Field Type:

      Type Holds
      Text Free text.
      Number A number, for example a budget.
      Date A date, for example a renewal date.
      Boolean Yes or no.
      Select A value from a list you define under Custom Lists.

      Tick Allow multiple values when a contact can have several, for example several preferred stores.

    3. Choose whether it applies to Contact, Company or both, under Entity, then Create Field.

    The field then appears on every contact's page and in the contact form, and can be shown as a column in the Details List with View.

    Deleting a field deletes its values

    Removing a custom field removes the values stored in it for every contact. It cannot be undone.

    Custom Lists¶

    The lists of values used by Select custom fields: statuses, categories, types. Example: a Store list with Jurong Point, Mid Valley and CentralWorld.

    1. Open the Custom Lists tab, then Add Reference List.
    2. Give it a Technical List Identifier (letters, digits, - and _, for example store) and a List Name.
    3. Add the entries with Add Entry, each with a code and a label. Drag them to reorder, or sort them alphabetically.

    Write names and labels in English: GoodBards translates them automatically into the other languages of the app.

    Import Jobs¶

    Every spreadsheet import, with its status and how many rows were processed, so you can check that an import went through. Start a new one with Bulk Add Contacts. See Adding contacts.

    Good to know¶

    • Segments update themselves; mailing lists do not. A segment re-evaluates its rule each time; a mailing list stays as you built it.
    • Watch your plan limit. The Total Contacts bar shows how close you are.
    • Respect unsubscribes. Under Singapore's PDPA and equivalent rules elsewhere, contacts who opt out must not receive marketing email. GoodBards excludes them, but do not work around it by re-importing them.

    Still stuck? Contact support.
