    Emails¶

    Design, send and track the emails you send to your contacts — from a one-off announcement to a regular newsletter.

    What it's for¶

    Email is where most marketing plans still start. GoodBards keeps the whole job in one place: you pick a look, write the message, choose who receives it, and see how it performed — without exporting lists or switching to a separate email tool.

    Because your emails sit alongside your contacts, anything you already know about your audience is available when you choose recipients.

    Before you start¶

    • At least one contact in Audience → Contacts, or a mailing list to send to.
    • Your Brand Kit set up, if you want emails to pick up your colours and logo automatically. See Brand Kit.

    How to create an email¶

    1. In the sidebar, choose Marketing → Emails.
    2. Choose + Create Email.
    3. Pick how you want to start. GoodBards offers four options:

    Option When to use it
    Designs Gallery The usual choice. Reuse a look and feel — header, footer and branding — and just write the message. Marked Recommended.
    Import from External You already have the email elsewhere. Paste HTML, upload a .eml file, upload a .zip export (for example from Canva), or fetch a URL.
    Reuse existing from platform Send something you have written before, with fresh recipients.
    Start Blank An empty email with no styling. Best for a plain, personal message.
    1. Work through the three steps at the top of the dialog: Look & Feel, then Content, then Recipients.
    2. Preview your email before sending, and send yourself a test if you can.

    Reuse beats starting over

    If you send a similar email regularly, Reuse existing from platform keeps the content and design and only asks for new recipients — usually faster than building it again.

    Importing a design from Canva¶

    Canva exports an email as a .zip holding the email's HTML and its images.

    1. In Canva, export your email design. Keep the .zip as it is; there's no need to unzip it.
    2. In Create Email, choose Import from External, then the ZIP (Canva) tab, and drop the .zip. Any .zip with one HTML file and its images works the same way.
    3. Choose Analyze & clean up. The report lists what the file contained: the images found, any image the HTML uses but the .zip lacks, and files that won't be used.
    4. Check the Import name. It names the email and the folder its images go to.
    5. Continue through Scope and Images. The images are copied to Drive › My Documents › Images › your import name and made public so recipients can see them; the email points at those copies.

    Good to know:

    • The plain-text version of the email is created from the HTML, with its links. The .txt file in Canva's export is not used.
    • Canva's unsubscribe link is converted automatically. If an email has no unsubscribe link, the report warns you: add one, or use a Design whose footer has it, before sending.
    • If the design uses a font recipients don't have (Canva's brand fonts, for example), the report names it and the font they'll see instead.
    • SVG images are not imported: Gmail and Outlook don't display them. Export them from Canva as PNG instead.

    Editing an email¶

    Choose an email's preview in the gallery to open it. Its page shows the subject and the body as recipients will see it, with an Edit button on each, and Send Test Email.

    Edit on the body opens the editor. The design (header and footer) is locked: you edit only your content inside it.

    • Visual: click any text to change it; drag blocks from the right (heading, text, button, image, two columns, divider…). Personal fields such as First name are filled in for each recipient.
    • Code: edit the HTML directly.
    • AI: describe the change, for example make it shorter and warmer. You can also paste links: write a newsletter from these posts followed by up to three web addresses builds a section per post, with a "Read more" link to each. The pages are read before the AI writes, and the panel tells you which ones it could read; a page whose owner blocks automated access is named, not guessed at.
    • Desktop, Email width and Mobile preview the email at each size.

    Choose Apply to keep your changes, or Cancel.

    The card menu in the gallery also offers Show Tactics (where the email is used), Duplicate, Attach to tactic and Delete.

    Attaching an email to a tactic¶

    An email is sent from a campaign tactic: the tactic holds the audience, the sender and the send button. Attach to tactic in the card menu links the email to one, in three ways:

    • Existing tactic: pick a campaign, then one of its email tactics. Only tactics that can still take the email are listed: a tactic sends one email, so those that already sent theirs, and closed tactics, are left out. A tactic whose email is still a draft, failed, or scheduled is listed with that email's name; attaching replaces it after you confirm. The audience and the sender stay; a scheduled send is cancelled, and you schedule the new email again from the tactic page.
    • New tactic in a campaign: pick a campaign and name the tactic; it is created as a draft with the email attached.
    • New campaign: name the campaign, choose its objective and name the tactic; both are created with the email attached.

    Each option opens the tactic page, where you set the recipients and send.

    Finding your emails¶

    Everything you have created is listed under Email Gallery, on the Emails tab. Use the search box to find one by name, and the buttons beside it to switch between grid and list view.

    The Email Designs tab holds the reusable looks — headers, footers and branding — rather than individual messages.

    Good to know¶

    • Emails and designs are separate. A design is the wrapper you reuse; an email is one message you send. Editing a design does not rewrite emails already sent.
    • Check the preview before every send. Email clients vary, and a layout that looks right in the editor can shift in Gmail or Outlook.
    • Sent emails cannot be recalled. Confirm your recipient list before the final step.

    Still stuck? Contact support.
