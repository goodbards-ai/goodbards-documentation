    Frequently Asked Questions¶

    Account & access¶

    How do I reset my password?¶

    Go to the login page and choose Forgot Password?. You will receive an email with a reset link.

    How do I change my name, language or profile picture?¶

    In Personal Settings, reached from your profile picture at the top right. See Personal Settings.

    Can I turn on two-factor authentication?¶

    Yes — under Personal Settings → Password & Security. Worth doing: a marketing account can usually email your entire contact database.

    Why can I not see a section other people mention?¶

    What appears in your sidebar depends on your plan and your permissions. Agentic AI in particular is not included in every plan.

    Permissions¶

    Most "the button is missing" or "the button is greyed out" questions come down to one of two things: your role (which modules you can change), or the Access sensitive data permission (whether you can handle personal details). Hover a greyed-out button to see which one is missing. Both are set by an administrator in Tenant Settings → Security. See Users, roles & permissions.

    Why is Add Contact greyed out for me?¶

    Adding a contact needs the Access sensitive data permission, because the contact form holds email, phone and address. The button says Requires the Access sensitive data permission.

    This permission is given to a person, not to a role, so being a Marketer, an administrator or even the owner is not enough on its own. An administrator gives it in Tenant Settings → Security → Users: the pencil in your Permissions column, then tick Access sensitive data under Additional permissions.

    Only the Sales role includes it automatically.

    Why can I not create a segment?¶

    Creating, editing and deleting segments needs write access to the Audience module, which the Marketer and Sales roles include. You do not need to be an administrator, and you do not need Access sensitive data: without it you can still build segments, the contacts' emails and phones are simply hidden inside them.

    If Add Segment is missing, your role gives you read-only access to the Audience. Ask an administrator to give you the Marketer role, or a custom role with Audience write.

    Segments used to be administrator-only

    Until November 2026 creating a segment required the Tenant Administrator role. That restriction is gone: any Audience writer can now manage segments.

    Why can I not import contacts or manage mailing lists?¶

    Bulk Add Contacts (CSV import), creating a mailing list and adding or removing its members need both the administrator role and Access sensitive data. These change many contacts at once, so they stay with administrators. A Marketer sees the button greyed out with the reason.

    Why are contacts' emails and phone numbers hidden?¶

    You do not have Access sensitive data. Without it, personal details are hidden everywhere: lists, contact pages, segments, exports and the assistant's answers. It is deliberate — under Singapore's PDPA and similar laws, give it only to people who work with contacts directly.

    I was given a permission but nothing changed¶

    Permissions apply at your next login, or when you switch workspace. Log out and back in.

    Why do I not receive the Daily Pulse email or chatbot lead alerts?¶

    Each is sent only to people who have the matching additional permission: Recipient for daily report emails and Recipient for lead capture alert emails. If nobody in your company has it, nobody gets the email. See Additional permissions.

    I can only see some contacts. Where are the others?¶

    Your company has turned on Restrict contacts to their owners, and your role (usually Sales) sees only the contacts and companies you own. Ask an administrator to assign you as an owner, or to change your role. See Contact ownership.

    Setting up¶

    What should I set up first?¶

    Brand & Context, then your Brand Kit, then Connections, then contacts. See Sign Up & Onboarding for why that order.

    Why is there no data on my analytics pages?¶

    Almost always a missing connector. Check Connections — pages such as Google Analytics and Google Trends read from your own accounts and show nothing until those are linked.

    I switched workspace in one tab, but my other tabs did not follow¶

    That is deliberate. Each browser tab keeps its own workspace and its own assistant conversation, so you can work on two clients side by side. A new tab opens on the workspace you used last.

    Some pages take a while to load. Is that normal?¶

    Yes. Screens that pull live data from Google can take several seconds. An empty chart usually means it is still loading rather than that there is no data.

    Content & AI¶

    Does the AI know about my business?¶

    It knows what you tell it in Brand & Context — what your company does, how it sounds, who it competes with. Filling that in properly is the single biggest improvement you can make to AI output.

    Can I control what the AI can see?¶

    Yes. Each field in Brand & Context has a Visible to AI / Hide From AI toggle, so you can keep commercially sensitive details out of generated content.

    Why could a web page not be read?¶

    Some sites tell automated tools not to read their pages, through a file called robots.txt. GoodBards respects it: such pages are skipped with the message The site owner does not allow automated access to this page. Copy the text you need and paste it in instead.

    Which AI model is used?¶

    Set per generation type — text, image, video and audio — in AI Settings. The defaults work well; change them only if you have a specific reason.

    Does generating content cost anything?¶

    It consumes credits from your monthly allowance. Video costs more than images, which cost more than text. Track it in Usage & Billing.

    Campaigns & publishing¶

    What is the difference between a tactic and a campaign?¶

    A tactic is one piece of marketing on one channel — a single post, one email. A campaign groups several tactics towards one goal over a period of time. If you just want to publish one post, you want a tactic. See Launch a Tactic.

    Why is my ad campaign not spending anything?¶

    New campaigns start paused, and need at least one enabled ad. See Digital Ads.

    Can I recall an email after sending it?¶

    No. Check your recipient list and send yourself a test first — see Emails.

    Audience & data¶

    What happens if someone unsubscribes?¶

    They move to the unsubscribed group and stop receiving marketing email, whether you send to chosen contacts, a segment or a mailing list. Do not work around this by re-importing them — under Singapore's PDPA and equivalent rules elsewhere, opt-outs must be respected. See Unsubscribed contacts.

    How do I find contacts who stopped engaging, and keep them out of a send?¶

    There is no automatic "unengaged" status. Each contact has a decay score that fades to zero over the period your administrator sets (4, 8 or 12 weeks). Create a segment on Decay Score less than 1 to find them, or on the opens and clicks of the last N days for a rule of your own, and send to a segment that leaves them out. See Unengaged contacts.

    What is the difference between a segment and a mailing list?¶

    A segment is a saved rule, re-evaluated each time you use it, so it stays current. A mailing list is a fixed set of contacts. See Contacts.

    Can I keep separate workspaces for different clients?¶

    Yes, if your plan includes sub-workspaces. Each has its own contacts, campaigns and credit allowance. See Sub-Workspaces.
