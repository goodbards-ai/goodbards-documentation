    Branches and conditions¶

    Send different contacts down different lanes: those who clicked and those who did not, retail and education, hot and cold leads.

    What it's for¶

    A branch checks something about the contact and splits the flow into lanes, one per answer. The lanes sit side by side on the canvas and join again below, so the steps after the branch run for everyone, unless a lane ends with Exit flow.

    In short

    You want Use
    Two lanes: yes or no Yes / No branch
    Three or more lanes, checked in order Routing
    To compare two paths on a random share, for example 50/50 Random split
    The AI to judge, for example hot / warm / cold AI decides
    Some contacts to stop here A lane that ends with Exit flow
    Some contacts to skip a step An empty lane

    Yes / No branch¶

    One condition, two lanes: contacts who match it, and everyone else.

    1. Add Yes / No branch where the flow should split.
    2. In the side panel, name the first lane under Lane label and pick its Condition.
    3. Name the Last lane (everyone else).
    4. Add steps inside each lane with its +.

    Sample: Tutorial 2 · Welcome series.

    Routing¶

    Several rules, checked from left to right: the first that holds opens its lane. Contacts who match none take the last lane.

    Choose Add Rule in the panel for each lane, and reorder rules with the ↑ ↓ arrows: order matters when a contact could match two rules.

    Sample: Tutorial 3 · Route by interest.

    Random split¶

    Share the contacts at random between 2 to 4 lanes, to compare two paths: a designed email against a plain one, a discount against free shipping, a 2-day wait against a 5-day one.

    1. Drag Random split from the palette onto the canvas. It starts with two lanes, A · 50% and B · 50%.
    2. In the panel, give each lane a name and a share. Each share is at least 5% and the shares add up to 100%; Even split fills in 50/50, 34/33/33 or 25 each. Add a lane goes up to four.
    3. Each lane starts with an Add tag step, split: A and split: B, so you can find the contacts of a lane later, in a segment or a report. Rename the tag (for example ab: w0-design) or delete the step.
    4. Put the steps you want to compare in each lane, for example a different email.

    Good to know:

    • The draw is random, but it sticks. A contact keeps their lane: a retry, or entering the flow again, lands them in the same one.
    • Shares are close, not exact, on small numbers. With 1,000 contacts a 50/50 split lands between 45% and 55% per lane; with 20 contacts it can be 13 and 7.
    • Two splits in one flow draw separately. Being in lane A of the first says nothing about the second.
    • Nothing is picked for you. The split never moves contacts to the better lane; you read the result and decide.

    Reading the result: on the flow's Report tab (see Results and analytics), each split has its own table: per lane, the contacts, how many were emailed, opened and clicked (real people only, a contact counted once), and a line per measure:

    • "Clicks: A is ahead (15% against 7.5% for B), 95% sure": the gap is too large to be luck;
    • "… but it is too close to call": the gap could be luck, wait for more contacts;
    • with fewer than 100 contacts emailed, a note says to treat it as a hint.

    A lane needs an email step for its figures; a split with fewer than two such lanes shows contacts only.

    AI decides¶

    The AI answers one question about the contact and picks a lane. See AI and human steps.

    The conditions¶

    The same conditions work in branches, routing, goals and stop rules:

    Condition True when
    Has tag The contact has the tag. Several tags: any or all of them.
    Field value A contact field is equal to, not equal to, contains, is one of, is empty, is not empty, greater or less than a value. You pick the field from the same list as in segments: contact fields (city, country, Customer Type, preferred language, timezone, created on, birth date…) and your custom fields. Pick-list fields offer their values. Dates compare as YYYY-MM-DD, for example created on greater than 2026-01-01.
    Score Under Field value, group Scores: Engagement Score or Decay Score (the same, faded with time: "engaged lately"), for example Decay Score greater than 50. They are the two scores of the contact page.
    In segment The contact is a member of the segment right now.
    Opened or clicked an email The contact opened (or clicked) an email sent earlier in this flow. With Clicked ticked, Link narrows it to one named link: "clicked the kit button" rather than any link. See Naming a link.
    Outcome of a task A Task for the owner earlier in the flow was done, dismissed or timed out.
    What an earlier step wrote A value an agent, a task or an AI decision stored for this contact.
    An answer in the form Flows started by a form: an answer in the submission that started the flow, for example form.rating less than 3. Pick it under What an earlier step wrote.

    Owner, mailing list, consent, tags

    These are not in the field list. Use Has tag, or build a segment (owner, mailing list, "can be messaged on") and use In segment.

    Customer Type

    Customer Type is the B2B / B2C choice of the contact page. It does not say whether the contact bought. For that, use a tag (for example status: customer) or a pick-list custom field.

    Survey answers

    Use An answer in the form rather than a custom field to branch on a survey score. A form fills a custom field only when it is empty, so a contact's second survey would still show the first score.

    Exit lanes and empty lanes¶

    • A lane that ends with Exit flow takes its contacts out: the lanes that join again below are the others.

    • An empty lane means "skip": its contacts go straight to the step after the branch. Sample: Tutorial 5.

    Good to know¶

    • Checked once, when the contact reaches the branch. A branch does not wait: put a Wait for open / click before a branch on an email.
    • Lanes can hold branches, but keep flows readable: past three levels, split into two flows connected by a tag.
    • Stop anywhere is not a branch: for "stop as soon as they buy", use a stop rule in Flow settings.
