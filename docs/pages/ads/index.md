# Digital Ads

Plan paid search and follow what your ads bring. In the app, **Digital Ads**
sits under **Marketing** in the sidebar.

**Who sees it:** everyone your administrator gives the **Digital ads** access
to.

## The screens

### Digital Ads

Your ad campaigns and their results, next to the rest of your marketing.

See [Digital Ads](../marketing/digital-ads.md).

### Keyword planner

Research the keywords your market searches for, with volumes and costs, when your
Google Ads account is connected.
