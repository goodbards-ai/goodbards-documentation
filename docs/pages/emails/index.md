# Emails & Forms

Design, send and measure email, and collect sign-ups with forms. In the app,
both sit under **Marketing** in the sidebar.

**Who sees it:** everyone your administrator gives the **Emails and forms**
access to.

## The screens

### Emails

Your email gallery. Build an email in the visual editor, from a template or with
the AI, send it to a segment or mailing list, and follow opens, clicks and
bounces per recipient. Opens by mail-provider robots are left out, so the open
rate reflects people.

See [Emails](../marketing/emails.md).

### Email designs

The frame around your emails: header, footer, colours and fonts from your Brand
Kit. Pick a design once and every email, event email and form confirmation uses
it.

See [Email designs](../marketing/email-designs.md).

### Forms

Sign-up, registration and feedback forms, from the gallery or from scratch. Each
submission can create or update a contact, with the fields you map.

See [Forms](../marketing/forms.md).

## Where to start

Set up an email design first: every email you send afterwards looks like your
brand without extra work.
