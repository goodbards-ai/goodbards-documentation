# Audience

Everyone you market to: the people, the organisations they work for, and how
they engage with you.

**Who sees it:** everyone your administrator gives the **Audience** access to.
Depending on their role, a colleague may see only the contacts they own.

## The screens

### Contacts

The people you market to, with their details, custom fields, status and history
of every email, form and event. Group them into **segments** that update
themselves as contacts change.

See [Contacts](contacts.md).

### Companies

The organisations your contacts belong to, with their own segments.

See [Companies](companies.md).

### Mailing lists

Lists people subscribe to, for example from a sign-up form. A contact who
unsubscribes stays unsubscribed: adding them again by hand does not override
their choice.

See [Mailing lists](mailing-lists.md).

### Adding contacts

Add one contact, import a spreadsheet with **Bulk Add Contacts**, or photograph a
business card with **Scan Business Card**. **Import Jobs** shows how each import
went.

See [Adding contacts](adding-contacts.md).

### Lead scoring

A 0–100 score per contact, from what they did: opened, clicked, registered,
attended. Use it to see who is ready for a sales conversation.

See [Lead scoring](lead-scoring.md).

## Why this comes first

Almost everything else depends on your audience. Emails need recipients, forms
create contacts, and reporting is only as good as the data behind it. If you are
setting up GoodBards for the first time, get contacts in before building
campaigns.
