# Mailing lists

Lists people subscribe to, such as your newsletter or a members' club, with
their consent kept for each list.

## What it's for

A segment is a rule; a mailing list is a commitment. When someone joins a list,
from a sign-up form or because you added them, they agreed to receive what that
list sends. GoodBards keeps each person's status per list, so you always know who
may receive what, and an unsubscribe is respected everywhere.

## Before you start

- Contacts to add, or a [form](../marketing/forms.md) people can sign up with.

## How to manage a mailing list

1. In the sidebar, choose **Audience → Contacts**, then the **Mailing Lists**
   tab. Each list shows its description and number of members.

    ![The Mailing Lists tab: Tropika Rewards members, Gold & Silver members, Tropika Kids families and Corporate gifting buyers, with their members and creation date](../../img/screenshots/mailing-lists.png)

2. To create one, choose **New Mailing List**, give it a **Name** and a
   **Description**, pick the **Privacy statement** shown on its sign-up form,
   and choose **Create**. **Default owner of new contacts** gives everyone who
   signs up through this list to one colleague.

    ![The Create mailing list dialog: Name, Description, Default owner of new contacts, Privacy statement set to the company default, Cancel and Create](../../img/screenshots/mailing-list-create.png){ width="40%" }

3. Choose a list to open it. The counters show who is **Subscribed**,
   **Pending**, **Unsubscribed**, and **Not on list**.

    ![A mailing list opened: 743 subscribed, 0 pending, 63 unsubscribed and 2,111 not on the list, with Embed Form and Edit, above its members and their status](../../img/screenshots/mailing-list-members.png)

4. Below, each contact shows their contact status, their status on this list,
   and how they joined. **Add** puts a contact who is not on the list on it;
   **Revoke** takes a member off. Select several rows to add or revoke them at
   once.
5. **Embed Form** gives you a sign-up form for your own website that adds people
   to this list.

!!! info "Unsubscribed means locked"
    Someone who unsubscribed from a list cannot be added back, or removed, by
    anyone on your team. Only they can rejoin, from their preference page or a
    sign-up form. This is how GoodBards keeps you compliant with PDPA.

## Good to know

!!! warning "Deleting a list removes it from your emails"
    Deleting a mailing list permanently removes its memberships, and removes the
    list from any email that used it. The contacts themselves stay.

- **Lists do not update themselves.** A segment re-evaluates its rule every time;
  a mailing list only changes when people join or leave. See
  [Contacts](contacts.md#segments).
- **Email the list from a campaign.** When you send an email, choose the list as
  the audience. See [Emails](../marketing/emails.md).
