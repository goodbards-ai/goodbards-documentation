# Agentic AI

AI assistants that do marketing work for you — writing, researching, generating
images and audio.

## In this section

- **[Chat Agents](chat-agents.md)** — assistants you hold a conversation with,
  each set up for a particular kind of work.
- **[Workflow Agents](workflow-agents.md)** — agents that carry out a multi-step
  task on your behalf.
- **[Research Assistant](research-assistant.md)** — ask for research on a market,
  competitor or topic.
- **[Image Generation](image-generation.md)** — create images from a description.
- **[Video Generation](video-generation.md)** — create short video clips.
- **[Text to Speech](text-to-speech.md)** — turn written copy into spoken audio.

## Good to know

Agentic AI is not included in every plan. If this section does not appear in your
sidebar, your plan does not include agents.
