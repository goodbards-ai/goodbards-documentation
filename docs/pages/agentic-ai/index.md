# Agentic AI

AI assistants that do marketing work for you: writing, researching, generating
images and audio.

**Who sees it:** everyone your administrator gives the **AI studio** access to.

## Before you start

Chat Agents, Workflow Agents, Research Assistant and Agent Parameters are
included in the **Pro** and **Full** plans. Image Generation, Video Generation
and Text to Speech are also in the **Lite** plan. A screen your plan does not
include shows a crown in the sidebar.

## The screens

### Chat Agents

Assistants you hold a conversation with, each set up for a particular kind of
work.

See [Chat Agents](chat-agents.md).

### Workflow Agents

Agents that carry out a multi-step task on your behalf.

See [Workflow Agents](workflow-agents.md).

### Research Assistant

Ask for research on a market, competitor or topic.

See [Research Assistant](research-assistant.md).

### Image Generation

Create images from a description.

See [Image Generation](image-generation.md).

### Video Generation

Create short video clips.

See [Video Generation](video-generation.md).

### Text to Speech

Turn written copy into spoken audio.

See [Text to Speech](text-to-speech.md).

### Agent Parameters

The variables your agents' prompts can use, such as *Audience*,
*CallToAction* or *Boilerplate100*. Many are filled in for you from your
Brand & Context, such as your company boilerplate. The colour of each row shows
whether it is a global, system or local variable; **New Variable** adds your
own.

!!! tip "You can leave Agent Parameters alone"
    Most teams never need to change them.
