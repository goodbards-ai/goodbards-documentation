# Social Media

Publish to your social channels and see how those posts performed.

**Who sees it:** everyone your administrator gives the **Social media** access
to.

## Before you start

Social Media is included in the **Pro** and **Full** plans. Publishing and
reports need the channel connected first, under **Connectors**: see
[Connections](../settings/connections.md). Without it, there is nowhere for a
post to go.

## The screens

### Social Post

Write a post for LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, X or TikTok, with the AI's help for the
text and hashtags, then publish it now or schedule it. You can also turn an
article into a series of posts.

See [Social Post](social-post.md).

### Conversation Compass

All your networks on one page, mostly charts: followers, engagement, the best
time to post, where to invest and what works, with AI advice on request. It is
the first tab of **Analysis**.

See [Conversation Compass](conversation-compass.md).

### Analysis

How your social content performs on each channel: reach, engagement and
followers, post by post.

See [Analysis](analysis.md).

### Social reports

A report per social account and period, kept in a history and sent to you on
a schedule if you want.

See [Social reports](reports.md).
