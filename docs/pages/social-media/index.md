# Social Media

Publish to your social channels and see how those posts performed.

## In this section

- **[Social Post](social-post.md)** — write, schedule and publish posts to your
  connected accounts.
- **[Analysis](analysis.md)** — how your social content is performing across
  channels.

## Before you start

Social publishing needs the channel connected first. See
[Connections](../settings/connections.md) — without it, there is nowhere for a
post to go.
