# Momentum

Your pages that are gaining or losing ground right now, so you can act while the
trend is on your side.

## What it's for

A page that suddenly gets more attention is worth a push now, not next quarter:
a link from your newsletter, a social post, an update. A page that is losing
ground needs a look before it disappears. Momentum ranks your pages by how fast
they are moving.

## Before you start

- **Your plan.** Momentum is included in the **Pro** and **Full** plans.
- **Google Search Console** and **Google Analytics** connected under
  **Connectors**. See [Connections](../settings/connections.md).
- To write a follow-up article straight from Momentum: the article writer
  (**Content Workspace**) in your plan.

## How to read it

1. In the sidebar, choose **Content Hub → Momentum**.
2. Filter by stage: **Rising**, **Peaking**, **Plateau** or **Declining**.

![Momentum: the advice "Compound the gain" for a SaaS / Software product, the Rising, Peaking, Plateau and Declining filters, and two rising pages with a momentum of 99 and 93 and their trend lines](../../img/screenshots/seo-momentum.png)

Each card shows the page, its momentum score out of 100 with the change, the
trend line, and its clicks, position and impressions. Open a card for the search
queries behind it and the visitors' engagement.

## How to write a follow-up article

For a **Rising** or **Peaking** page, the AI can suggest follow-up articles built
on what people are searching for more and more on that page. It reads the page
first, so the articles stay on the page's subject.

1. Open the page's card.
2. Under **Next logical development**, choose **Suggest Articles**.
3. Read the suggestions. Each one shows a working title, a short description of
   the article, and the **rising searches** it is built on.

    ![Next logical development: two suggested follow-up articles, each with a working title, its upside and effort, a description, the rising search it answers, and the buttons Add To Action Queue and Create](../../img/screenshots/seo-momentum-follow-up.png){ width="65%" }

4. Choose **Create** on the one you want. The article writer opens with the
   title under **What is it about?** and the suggestion's description under
   **Instructions for the AI**.

    ![The article writer opened from a suggestion: the working title under What is it about? and the suggestion's description under Instructions for the AI](../../img/screenshots/seo-momentum-create.png)

5. Scroll down to **Sources and model**. Your page is already listed as a web
   page to read, and the rising searches as **Keywords**.

    ![The lower part of the article writer: the page listed under Import web pages, the rising search as a keyword, and the Write It button](../../img/screenshots/seo-momentum-create-sources.png)

6. Change anything you like, for example the angle under **Instructions for the
   AI**, then choose **Write It**.

Not ready to write? Choose **Add To Action Queue** on the suggestion instead, and
find it later under **Actions**.

!!! info "Credits are used only when you choose Write It"
    **Create** only opens the article writer with everything filled in. Nothing
    is written, and no credit is used, until you choose **Write It**.

The suggestions stay on the page until you choose **Suggest Again**.

!!! note "No Create button?"
    **Create** needs the article writer (**Content Workspace**) in your plan.
    Without it you can still choose **Add To Action Queue**.

## Good to know

- **Rising searches come from your own Search Console**: the searches this page
  gained over the last 28 days compared with the 28 days before. For a
  **News / Media** site the comparison is over 7 days, because news searches rise
  and fall within days. Check your site type under
  **Brand & Context → Website**.
- **The last 3 days are not counted yet.** Google Search Console publishes its
  figures about 3 days late, so a search that took off yesterday shows up in
  Momentum a few days later.
- **Searches for your company name are left out**, because an article about your
  own name rarely brings new readers. Make sure your company name is filled in
  under **Brand & Context → General Information**.
- **No suggestions?** If no search is growing on the page, there is nothing to
  build a follow-up on, and Momentum says so instead of guessing. The same
  happens when the growing searches mean something else than the page: a page
  about AI tools for founders can show up for "ai startup accelerator", which
  people mostly use for accelerator programmes.
- **Low confidence** means the growing searches add up to fewer than 50
  impressions. Treat those suggestions as ideas to check, not a plan.
- **Fill in your company description** under **Brand & Context → General
  Information**. Without it the AI only knows what the page says, and Momentum
  reminds you next to the suggestions.
- **The page could not be read?** If your site blocks it (robots.txt) or does
  not answer, the suggestions are built on the searches alone, and Momentum
  says so.
- **Plateau and Declining pages** get **Add Refresh Task** instead: a page that
  is losing ground needs an update, not a new article next to it.
- **Momentum counts all visits**, from search, social, email and other sites, not
  only Google search.
- **The advice at the top depends on your site type**, detected automatically;
  choose **Edit** to correct it.
- **For pages that rank but under-perform**, use
  [Page Fixes](page-fixes.md) instead.
