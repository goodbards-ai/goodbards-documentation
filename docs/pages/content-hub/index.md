# Content Hub

Write, store and reuse your content: articles, posts, press releases and the
files behind them.

**Who sees it:** everyone your administrator gives the **Content hub** access to.

## The screens

### Content Workspace

Write with an AI writer and reviewer. Give a short brief (topic, audience,
format); the writer drafts it in your brand voice, the reviewer scores it and
says what to improve, and you revise until it is ready to save.

See [Content Workspace](content-workspace.md).

### Research & Plan

What to write next: topics your market searches for, questions your customers
ask and gaps against competitors, each one ready to turn into a brief.

See [Research & Plan](research-plan.md).

### Content Repository

All your files and content in one place: documents, images, articles and posts,
in folders, searchable by their content.

See [Content Repository](../workspace/drive.md).

### Translator

Translate a text or a document while keeping its formatting.

See [Translator](../tool-box/translator.md).
