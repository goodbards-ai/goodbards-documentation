# What is GoodBards?

GoodBards is an **Agentic AI Marketing OS** — a single platform where AI agents
plan, create and execute marketing campaigns, while remembering your brand's
voice, data and history.

In practice that means the work you would normally spread across an email tool, a
social scheduler, a CRM and a folder of brand assets happens in one place, with AI
that already knows your business.

## What makes it different

These are the ideas the rest of the documentation builds on. Understanding them
early makes everything else make sense.

| | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Agentic Memory** | Agents remember. Personal, agent and organisation-wide memory mean every interaction builds on the last, rather than starting from nothing. |
| **Voice Bank** | Your brand's voice, captured once. Agents write like you rather than in a generic default. See [Brand & Context](../settings/company-settings.md). |
| **Brand Kit** | One source of truth for colours, fonts and logos, applied across every asset. See [Brand Kit](../settings/brand-kit.md). |
| **Content Generation** | Image, video and voice created natively — you do not leave the platform to produce an asset. |
| **Web Intelligence** | Scraping, scheduled crawls and feeds, so you can see what your market is doing. |
| **Connectors** | Links to the outside services you already use. See [Connections](../settings/connections.md). |

## The two words to know

**Tactic** — one piece of marketing on one channel: a single LinkedIn post, one
email. The smallest thing you can publish.

**Campaign** — several tactics working towards one goal over a period of time.

If you only want to publish one post, you want a tactic, not a campaign. See
[Launch a Tactic](../marketing/launch-tactic.md).

## Where to start

1. Set up [Brand & Context](../settings/company-settings.md) and your
   [Brand Kit](../settings/brand-kit.md) — everything the AI writes draws on them.
2. Connect the services you use, in [Connections](../settings/connections.md).
3. Add your [contacts](../audience/contacts.md).
4. Then publish something: [Launch a Tactic](../marketing/launch-tactic.md) or
   [send an email](../marketing/emails.md).

New to the interface? [Finding Your Way Around](navigating.md) is a tour of the
sidebar, screen by screen.
