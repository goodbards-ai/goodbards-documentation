# Finding Your Way Around

A short tour of the platform, so you know where each part of your marketing
lives.

## The sidebar

Everything is reached from the sidebar on the left. It is grouped by the kind of
work you are doing rather than by feature name.

| Section | What lives there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your overview — active work and how it is performing. |
| **Actions** | What to work on next. See [Actions](../workspace/actions.md). |
| **Drive** | Images, documents and files. See [Drive](../workspace/drive.md). |
| **Calendar** | Everything scheduled, on one timeline. See [Calendar](../workspace/calendar.md). |
| **Marketing** | Campaigns, emails, forms, events and tactics. |
| **Social Media** | Publishing to social channels, and how those posts performed. |
| **Agentic AI** | AI assistants that do marketing work for you. |
| **Audience** | Contacts, companies and partners. |
| **Website** | How your site is found and how visitors behave. |
| **Tool Box** | Small standalone utilities — translation, QR codes, short links. |

Sections with an arrow expand to show what is inside.

!!! note "Your sidebar may look different"
    What you see depends on your plan and your permissions. If a section
    described in this documentation is missing, your plan may not include it.

## The top bar

Along the top:

- **Your workspace name**, on the left. If you have access to more than one, this
  is where you switch between them.
- **Search**, in the middle — or press ++cmd+k++ (++ctrl+k++ on Windows) from
  anywhere.
- **Notifications**, the bell.
- **Language**, shown as a two-letter code such as **EN**.
- **Theme**, the sun or moon, switching between light and dark.
- **Your profile**, on the far right, leading to your personal settings.

## Where things are that people look for

| Looking for | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Send an email | **Marketing → Emails** |
| Post to social media | **Social Media → Social Post** |
| Add or import contacts | **Audience → Contacts** |
| Connect a social or analytics account | **Settings → Connections** |
| Set your logo, colours and brand voice | **Settings → Brand Kit** |
| Invite a colleague | **Settings → Users & Access** |
| See what is scheduled | **Calendar** |

## Two words worth knowing

**Tactic** — one piece of marketing on one channel: a single LinkedIn post, one
email. The smallest thing you can publish. See
[Launch a Tactic](../marketing/launch-tactic.md).

**Campaign** — several tactics working towards one goal over a period of time.
See [Campaigns](../marketing/campaigns.md).

If you only want to publish one post, you want a tactic, not a campaign.
