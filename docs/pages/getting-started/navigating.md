# Finding Your Way Around

A short tour of the platform, so you know where each part of your marketing
lives.

## The sidebar

Everything is reached from the sidebar on the left. It is grouped by the kind of
work you are doing rather than by feature name.

| Section | What lives there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your overview: the Daily Pulse, active work and how it is performing. See [Home](../home/home.md). |
| **Actions** | What to work on next. See [Actions](../workspace/actions.md). |
| **Content Hub** | Write and plan content: Content Workspace, Research & Plan, Momentum, Content Repository and Translator. |
| **Marketing** | Campaigns, emails, forms, events and digital ads. |
| **Social Media** | Publishing to social channels, and how those posts performed. |
| **Agentic AI** | AI assistants that do marketing work for you. |
| **Audience** | Contacts and companies. |
| **Organic Visibility** | How your site is found in search and AI assistants, and how visitors behave. |
| **Tool Box** | Utilities: web scraping, the website chatbot, QR codes, short links, the scheduler, the image editor. |

Sections with an arrow expand to show what is inside.

!!! note "Your sidebar may look different"
    What you see depends on your plan and your permissions. If a section
    described in this documentation is missing, your plan may not include it.

## The top bar

Along the top:

- **Your workspace name**, on the left. If you have access to more than one, this
  is where you switch between them.
- **Your shortcuts**, the icons before the search. By default
  **Content Repository** and **Calendar** (see
  [Content Repository](../workspace/drive.md) and
  [Calendar](../workspace/calendar.md)). Pick your own, up to six, in
  [Personal Settings → Shortcuts](../settings/personal-settings.md#shortcuts):
  for example **Scan Business Card** if you meet customers on the road.
- **Search**, the magnifying glass — or press ++cmd+k++ (++ctrl+k++ on Windows)
  from anywhere.
- **Notifications**, the bell: what happened while you were away. Switch
  between **All**, **Unread** and
  **Read**, and mark one as unread to come back to it.
- **The AI assistant**, the sparkle button.
- **Language**, shown as a two-letter code such as **EN**.
- **Theme**, the sun or moon, switching between light and dark.
- **Your account menu**, your picture on the far right: Personal Settings, Brand
  & Context, Tenant Settings, AI Settings, Connections, Tenant Usage and Platform
  Tutorials.

**Platform Tutorials** are guided tours inside the app: choose one, and it walks
you through a screen step by step, for example creating a chat agent.

## Where things are that people look for

| Looking for | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Send an email | **Marketing → Emails** |
| Post to social media | **Social Media → Social Post** |
| Add or import contacts | **Audience → Contacts** |
| Connect a social or analytics account | Account menu → **Connections** |
| Set your logo, colours and brand voice | Account menu → **Brand & Context** |
| Invite a colleague | Account menu → **Tenant Settings → Security** |
| See what is scheduled | **Marketing → Calendar**, or the Calendar icon in the top bar |
| Put a screen you use every day in the top bar | Account menu → **Personal Settings → Shortcuts** |
| Write an article or a post with AI | **Content Hub → Content Workspace** |

## Two words worth knowing

**Tactic** — one piece of marketing on one channel: a single LinkedIn post, one
email. The smallest thing you can publish. See
[Launch a Tactic](../marketing/launch-tactic.md).

**Campaign** — several tactics working towards one goal over a period of time.
See [Campaigns](../marketing/campaigns.md).

If you only want to publish one post, you want a tactic, not a campaign.
