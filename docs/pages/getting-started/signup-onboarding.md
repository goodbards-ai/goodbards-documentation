# Sign Up & Onboarding

Getting into GoodBards for the first time, and what to set up before you start
work.

## Creating your account

Your free workspace is ready as soon as your email is confirmed. There are two
ways to get there.

### With Google (fastest)

1. Go to [live.goodbards.ai](https://live.goodbards.ai) and choose **Sign Up**.
2. Choose **Sign up with Google** and pick your Google account.
3. Check your first and last name, add your job title and company name.
4. Choose **Create my workspace**. You land on your Home page, already signed in.

!!! info "No confirmation email"
    Google has already confirmed your address, so there is no link to click.
    Next time, use **Sign in with Google** on the login page. If you also want a
    password, use **Forgot password?** on the login page to set one.

!!! tip "Already invited by a colleague?"
    Accept the invitation from your email first. You then join their workspace
    instead of getting a new one of your own.

### With your email

1. Go to [live.goodbards.ai](https://live.goodbards.ai) and choose **Sign Up**.
2. Fill in your name, job title, company name and email address.
3. Open the email we send you and confirm your address.
4. We then email you a temporary password to log in with.

!!! warning "Disposable email addresses"
    Throwaway mailboxes (mailinator and the like) are not accepted, whichever way
    you sign up.

!!! note "Already have an account?"
    Go straight to the [login page](https://live.goodbards.ai/login). If you have
    forgotten your password, **Forgot Password?** below the password field sends
    you a reset link.

## Signing in

The login screen also carries three controls worth knowing about, along the top:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **SG** | Your region. |
| **EN** | Interface language. |
| Sun / moon | Switches between light and dark appearance. |

You can change all three later from inside the app. See
[Personal Settings](../settings/personal-settings.md).

## What to set up first

A new workspace is empty. Five things make everything else work better, roughly
in this order:

1. **[Brand & Context](../settings/company-settings.md)**: tell GoodBards what
   your company does. Everything the AI writes draws on this.
2. **[Brand Kit](../settings/brand-kit.md)**: your colours, fonts and logo, so
   emails and images come out on-brand automatically.
3. **[Connections](../settings/connections.md)**: link your social accounts and
   analytics. Most "why is there no data?" questions trace back to a missing
   connector.
4. **[Contacts](../audience/contacts.md)**: import the people you market to.
   Without contacts there is nobody to send to.
5. **Your team**: invite your colleagues and choose what each one can see and
   change. See [Users, roles & permissions](../settings/users-roles.md).

!!! tip "Do the brand setup before generating content"
    Agents use whatever is in Brand & Context at the moment they write. Filling it
    in afterwards does not improve drafts you have already produced.
