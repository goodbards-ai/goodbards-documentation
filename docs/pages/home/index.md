# Home

Your starting point each day: what changed, what needs doing, and what is
coming up.

**Who sees it:** everyone your administrator gives the **Home** access to.

## The screens

### Home

Your dashboard. The **Daily Pulse** sums up the last day for your company:
upcoming events and yesterday's registrations, how your contacts engaged, and
your readiness and engagement scores with their trend. Each card links to the
screen where you can act on it.

See [Home and the Daily Pulse](home.md).

### Actions

Your to-do list, filled by GoodBards. Each daily check (website, emails, social,
events) turns what it finds into an action with the evidence and the expected
impact. Work through them, mark them done, or dismiss what does not apply; done
actions are measured afterwards so you can see what paid off.

See [Actions](../workspace/actions.md).

### Calendar

Everything scheduled in one place: campaign tactics, social posts, emails and
events, by day, week or month.

See [Calendar](../workspace/calendar.md).

## Where to start

Open **Home** each morning, then **Actions**. If Actions is empty, the daily
checks have not found anything yet; connect your website and social accounts
under [Connections](../settings/connections.md) to give them something to read.
