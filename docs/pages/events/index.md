# Events

Run an event from the invitation to the follow-up: registration page, emails,
waitlist, check-in and results. In the app, **Event Management** sits under
**Marketing** in the sidebar.

**Who sees it:** everyone your administrator gives the **Events** access to.

## The screens

### Event Management

Your events as cards, with their status, date and registrations. Open one to work
through its tabs: Definition, Landing page, Registration, Communications,
Attendance, Analytics and Follow-up.

See [Event Management](../marketing/event-management.md).

### The event page

The page guests register on. It builds itself from the event's definition; make
a copy to lay it out your own way.

See [The event page](landing-page.md).

### Event emails

The invitation, reminders, confirmation and follow-up emails: put your brand on
all of them at once, reword one, or send an email of your own instead.

See [Event emails](emails.md).

### Check-in and badges

On the day: scan guests' QR codes at the door, or let them check in themselves at
a kiosk, and print name badges.

See [Check-in and badges](check-in.md).

### Ticket types and waitlist

Offer several ticket types or time slots, each with its own capacity, and decide
whether freed seats go to the waitlist automatically or by hand.

See [Ticket types and waitlist](tickets-waitlist.md).
