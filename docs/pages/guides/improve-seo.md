# Improve your presence with SEO

<span class="gb-soon">Guide coming soon</span>

Get found by people searching for what you do, in search engines and in AI
assistants.

## The stages

1. **Connect your site**: Google Search Console and Google Analytics. See
   [Connections](../settings/connections.md).
2. **See where you stand**: clicks, positions and trends. See
   [Organic Visibility](../website/index.md).
3. **Fix the pages that matter most**, from Page Fixes and your Actions. See
   [Actions](../workspace/actions.md).
4. **Write what your market searches for**, with Research & Plan and the Content
   Workspace. See [Content Hub](../content-hub/index.md).
5. **Check that AI assistants can find and quote you.**
6. **Measure the effect** a few weeks later.
