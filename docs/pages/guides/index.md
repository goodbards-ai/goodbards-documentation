# Guides

Step-by-step guides for the jobs that use several parts of GoodBards together.
Each one follows a real piece of work from start to finish and links to the
screens it uses.

## The guides

### [Run an event from start to finish](run-an-event.md)

Set up the event, invite your audience, manage registrations and the waitlist,
check guests in on the day, and follow up afterwards.

### [Run an email marketing campaign](email-campaign.md)

Choose who to reach, write and design the email, send or schedule it, and read
the results.

### [Create a social post from scratch](create-social-post.md)

Write one post by hand, save it as an asset, add it to a campaign, and publish
or schedule it.

### [Run a campaign on social media](social-campaign.md)

Plan a series of posts, write them with the AI in your brand voice, schedule
them, and see what worked.

### [Improve your presence with SEO](improve-seo.md)

Connect your site, find the pages worth fixing, write the content your market
searches for, and measure the effect.

## For developers and power users

### [Use the GoodBards API](use-the-api.md)

Connect your own tools: push contacts in, pull content and results out.

### [Use GoodBards from your AI assistant (MCP)](use-mcp.md)

Ask your GoodBards agents to work for you from Claude or another AI assistant.
