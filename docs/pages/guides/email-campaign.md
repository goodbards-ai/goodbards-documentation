# Run an email marketing campaign

<span class="gb-soon">Guide coming soon</span>

Choose who to reach, write and design the email, send it, and read what
happened.

## The stages

1. **Decide who receives it**: a segment or a mailing list. See
   [Contacts](../audience/contacts.md).
2. **Set up the campaign**: its goal and dates. See
   [Campaigns](../marketing/campaigns.md).
3. **Write and design the email**, with the AI in your brand voice. See
   [Emails](../marketing/emails.md).
4. **Test, then send or schedule it.**
5. **Read the results**: opens, clicks, bounces and who to follow up.
