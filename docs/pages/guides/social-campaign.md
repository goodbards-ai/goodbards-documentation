# Run a campaign on social media

<span class="gb-soon">Guide coming soon</span>

Plan a series of posts, write them in your brand voice, schedule them, and see
what worked.

## The stages

1. **Connect your accounts.** See [Connections](../settings/connections.md).
2. **Set up the campaign**: its goal and dates. See
   [Campaigns](../marketing/campaigns.md).
3. **Write the posts** with the AI, or turn an article into a series. See
   [Social Post](../social-media/social-post.md).
4. **Schedule them** and check the calendar. See
   [Calendar](../workspace/calendar.md).
5. **Read the results** per post and per network. See
   [Analysis](../social-media/analysis.md).
