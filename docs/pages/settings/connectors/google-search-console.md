# Google Search Console

Bring your Google search results — clicks, impressions, positions and queries —
into GoodBards.

## What it's for

The **Google Search Console** connector is the main source for GoodBards' search
screens:

- **Performance & Report**: clicks, impressions, position and click rate, alerts,
  page inspection and **Diagnostics**. See
  [Performance & Report](../../website/performance-report.md).
- **Page Fixes**: the pages to fix first. See [Page Fixes](../../website/page-fixes.md).
- **AI Readiness & Authority**. See [AI Readiness](../../website/ai-readiness.md).
- **Momentum**: which pages are gaining or losing search traffic. See
  [Momentum](../../website/momentum.md).
- **Research & Plan**: a plan needs Search Console connected and a site chosen.
  See [Research & Plan](../../content-hub/research-plan.md).
- **Digital Ads**: your top search queries, and the **Search Console** source when
  you choose keywords. See [Digital Ads](../../marketing/digital-ads.md).
- **The daily summary** on your home page.
- **AI agents** with the Search Console tool.

## Before you start

- You must be an **administrator** or the **owner** of your GoodBards company
  account.
- Your website must be a **verified property** in Google Search Console, and the
  Google account you use must have access to it.

## How to connect

1. Open your account menu (your picture, top right), then choose **Connections**.
2. On the **Web Analytics** tab, find **Google Search Console** and choose
   **Connect**.
3. Pick your Google account and allow GoodBards to **view your Search Console
   data**.
4. Back in GoodBards, choose **Manage Configuration** on the card.
5. Under **Search Console Site**, pick your website, then choose **Save
   Settings**.

## What GoodBards does with it

| GoodBards… | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Reads** | Your sites, search results by date, query, page, device and country, page inspection results and your sitemaps. |
| **Does not** | Change anything in Search Console. |

## Changing the site

Picking a different site is not a simple switch. GoodBards asks you to confirm
first (**Change your Search Console site?**), because:

- unfinished actions queued for the old site are closed, and the dialog tells you
  how many;
- what GoodBards learned from the old site — its content plans, Momentum moves,
  reports and its detected site type — no longer applies.

Choose **Keep current site** if you are not sure.

## Good to know

- **One site per workspace.** Every search screen uses the site you picked.
- **Data is two to three days behind.** That is how Google provides it.
- **Page inspections are limited** by Google to 2,000 a day per site.

## How to disconnect

1. On the Google Search Console card, choose **Manage Configuration**.
2. Choose **Disconnect**.

GoodBards forgets the access and the chosen site. To remove access on Google's
side too, do it from your Google Account, under third-party connections.
