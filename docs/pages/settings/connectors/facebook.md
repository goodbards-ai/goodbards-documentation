# Facebook

Publish posts to your Facebook Page and get reports on how it performs.

## What it's for

The **Facebook** connector links GoodBards to one of your Facebook Pages. With it
you can:

- **Publish and schedule posts** from a campaign's social tactic. See
  [Launch a tactic](../../marketing/launch-tactic.md) and
  [Social Post](../../social-media/social-post.md).
- **Link a post you published outside GoodBards**, so its results count in the
  campaign report.
- **Analyse your Page**: followers, post results and comments. See
  [Social Media Analysis](../../social-media/analysis.md) and
  [Reports](../../social-media/reports.md).
- **Schedule recurring reports** with the [Scheduler](../../tool-box/scheduler.md).
- **Feed the daily summary** on your home page.
- **Let your AI agents** list your Pages and read or create analysis reports.

## Before you start

- You must be an **administrator** or the **owner** of your GoodBards company
  account.
- Use a Facebook profile that **manages the Page** you want to publish to.

## How to connect

1. Open your account menu (your picture, top right), then choose **Connections**.
2. On the **Social Media** tab, find **Facebook** and choose **Connect**.
3. Sign in to Facebook. When Facebook asks which Pages GoodBards may use,
   **select the Pages you want** — Pages you leave out will not appear in
   GoodBards.
4. Back in GoodBards, choose **Manage configuration** on the Facebook card.
5. Under **Destination (one per tenant)**, pick your Page, then choose **Save
   configuration**.

If the list is empty, reconnect and make sure you select your Pages in step 3.

## What GoodBards does with it

| GoodBards… | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Publishes** | Posts on the chosen Page: text, text with a link preview, or text with one or several pictures. |
| **Deletes** | A published post, when you remove it from GoodBards. |
| **Reads** | Your Pages, their fans and followers, posts, post statistics, comments and daily Page statistics. |
| **Does not** | Publish videos, reels or stories, or read your messages. |

## Good to know

- **One Page per workspace.** The Page you pick is used for publishing *and* for
  the automated reports.
- **Picture or link preview, not both.** Without a picture, the first link in
  the text becomes a preview card.
- **Some figures are no longer available.** Facebook no longer provides
  impressions or reach for every post through its API.
- **Access expires.** When Facebook's access runs out, the connector switches off
  and you need to connect again.

## How to disconnect

1. On the Facebook card, choose **Manage configuration**.
2. Choose **Disconnect**, then confirm.

GoodBards forgets the access and the chosen Page. Posts already published stay on
Facebook. To remove GoodBards' access on Facebook's side too, do it from your
Facebook settings, under business integrations.
