# Google Trends

See how much people search for a topic, over time and by place.

## What it's for

The **Google Trends** connector reads Google's search-interest data. GoodBards
uses it in:

- **Organic Visibility → Google Trends**: compare search terms over time and by
  location. See [Google Trends](../../website/google-trends.md).
- **Research & Plan**: the demand signals behind a content plan. See
  [Research & Plan](../../content-hub/research-plan.md).
- **AI agents** with the Google Trends tool.

## Before you start

- You must be an **administrator** or the **owner** of your GoodBards company
  account.
- You need a Google account.

## How to connect

1. Open your account menu (your picture, top right), then choose **Connections**.
2. On the **Web Analytics** tab, find **Google Trends** and choose **Connect**.
3. Pick your Google account and accept the permissions, including access to
   search trends.
4. You are taken back to GoodBards. The card shows the account name.

## What GoodBards does with it

| GoodBards… | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Reads** | Search interest over time (by day, week, month or year) and by country, region, sub-region or city. |
| **Does not** | Change anything in your Google account. |

## Good to know

- **Not every place has a sub-region breakdown.** Google does not provide one for
  every location.
- **Recent days may be incomplete.** Google's latest figures are still settling.

## How to disconnect

1. On the Google Trends card, choose **Disconnect**.
2. Confirm.
