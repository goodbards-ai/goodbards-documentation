# LinkedIn Community

Tag people and companies with an **@mention** in the LinkedIn posts you write in
GoodBards.

!!! info "Now part of the LinkedIn connector"
    There is no separate LinkedIn Community card any more. Mentions now come
    with the main **LinkedIn** connector: connect it once, and **Mention…**
    appears in the post editor's **Insert** menu.

## What it's for

A mention puts your post in front of the person or company you tag. You search
for a person or a company page and GoodBards inserts the mention in your text.

## Before you start

- Connect [LinkedIn](linkedin.md). Nothing else is needed.

## How to mention someone

1. Write a LinkedIn post in the post editor. See
   [Social Post](../../social-media/social-post.md).
2. Open the **Insert** menu and choose **Mention…**.
3. Search for a person or a company, then pick them.

The mention is added to your text and becomes a real LinkedIn mention when the
post is published.

## Good to know

- **Mention… appears only when LinkedIn is connected**, and only when you write
  for LinkedIn.
- **Mentions already published stay on LinkedIn**, even if you disconnect.
