# LinkedIn Community

Tag people and companies with an **@mention** in the LinkedIn posts you write in
GoodBards.

## What it's for

A mention puts your post in front of the person or company you tag. This
connector adds a **Mention** button to the post editor when you write for
LinkedIn. You search for a person or a company page and GoodBards inserts the
mention in your text.

It does **not** publish. Publishing and analytics go through the main
[LinkedIn](linkedin.md) connector, so connect that one too.

## Before you start

- You must be an **administrator** or the **owner** of your GoodBards company
  account.
- Use a LinkedIn profile that has a role on your company page. The organisation
  you choose is used to search for people.

## How to connect

1. Open your account menu (your picture, top right), then choose **Connections**.
2. On the **Social Media** tab, find **LinkedIn Community** and choose
   **Connect**.
3. Sign in to LinkedIn and accept the permissions it asks for.
4. Back in GoodBards, choose **Manage configuration** on the card.
5. Under **Destination (one per tenant)**, pick your company page, then choose
   **Save configuration**.

## How to mention someone

1. Write a LinkedIn post in the post editor. See
   [Social Post](../../social-media/social-post.md).
2. Choose **Mention**.
3. Search for a person or a company, then pick them.

The mention is added to your text and becomes a real LinkedIn mention when the
post is published.

## What GoodBards does with it

| GoodBards… | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Reads** | People and company pages matching your search, and your organisations with their logos. |
| **Does not** | Publish, delete or read the statistics of posts. |

## Good to know

- **The Mention button appears only once this connector is connected**, and only
  when you write for LinkedIn.
- **Access is renewed automatically.** If LinkedIn refuses to renew it, the card
  asks you to reconnect.

## How to disconnect

1. On the LinkedIn Community card, choose **Manage configuration**.
2. Choose **Disconnect**, then confirm.

The **Mention** button disappears from the editor. Mentions already published stay
on LinkedIn.
