# YouTube

Bring your YouTube channel's results into GoodBards.

## What it's for

The **Google Youtube** connector reads your channel's statistics so you can see
them beside the rest of your marketing:

- **Analyse your channel**: views, watch time, likes, comments, shares,
  impressions and click rate for your recent videos. See
  [Social Media Analysis](../../social-media/analysis.md).
- **Feed the daily summary** on your home page.

It is **read-only**: GoodBards does not upload or publish videos to YouTube.

## Before you start

- You must be an **administrator** or the **owner** of your GoodBards company
  account.
- Use the Google account that **owns the channel**, or that is a **manager of the
  Brand Account** the channel belongs to.

!!! warning "Channels shared in YouTube Studio do not appear"
    If someone gave you access to a channel only through **YouTube Studio**
    permissions, Google does not let other apps read it. Ask for manager access
    to the Brand Account instead.

## How to connect

1. Open your account menu (your picture, top right), then choose **Connections**.
2. On the **Social Media** tab, find **Google Youtube** and choose **Connect**.
3. Pick your Google account. If Google asks, choose the **channel or Brand
   Account** you want to connect.
4. Accept the permissions: view your YouTube account and view its analytics
   reports.

## What GoodBards does with it

| GoodBards… | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Reads** | Your channel, its videos, their public counters and their analytics (views, watch time, engagement, impressions and click rate). |
| **Does not** | Upload, publish, edit or delete videos. |

## Good to know

- **Last 90 days.** A report covers videos from the last 90 days, up to 200
  videos.
- **Figures lag about two days.** Until YouTube's analytics catch up, GoodBards
  shows the public counters.
- **Impressions take a few days to appear.** The first time you connect, YouTube
  starts preparing impression reports for your channel. They start arriving
  after about two days, then fill in up to 30 days back.

## How to disconnect

1. On the Google Youtube card, choose **Disconnect**.
2. Confirm.

GoodBards forgets the access. To remove it on Google's side too, do it from your
Google Account, under third-party connections.
