# WordPress.com

Link a blog hosted on WordPress.com.

## What it's for

Use this connector if your blog is hosted by **WordPress.com**. If you run
WordPress on your own hosting, use [WordPress (self-hosted)](wordpress.md)
instead.

Once connected, your WordPress.com site is offered as a destination in a
campaign's **Blog Post** tactic. See [Launch a tactic](../../marketing/launch-tactic.md).

## Before you start

- You must be an **administrator** or the **owner** of your GoodBards company
  account.
- Use a WordPress.com account that can write posts on the site.

## How to connect

1. Open your account menu (your picture, top right), then choose **Connections**.
2. On the **CMS** tab, find **WordPress.com** and choose **Connect**.
3. Sign in to WordPress.com and approve GoodBards.
4. You are taken back to GoodBards. The card shows your site's name.

## What GoodBards does with it

| GoodBards… | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Creates** | A **draft** post with your title and content, with your picture as the featured image. |
| **Does not** | Publish the post for you — review and publish it in WordPress.com. |

## Good to know

- **Import WordPress** in the Content Repository works with self-hosted WordPress
  only.

## How to disconnect

1. On the WordPress.com card, choose **Disconnect**.
2. Confirm.

Drafts already sent stay on WordPress.com.
