# Chartbeat

Link your Chartbeat account, for real-time audience data on your websites.

## What it's for

Chartbeat measures, in real time, how readers use your site: top pages, referrers,
where readers are, headline tests and conversions. The **Chartbeat** connector
stores your Chartbeat key and the sites you want GoodBards to read.

!!! note "No screen shows Chartbeat data yet"
    You can connect Chartbeat today. GoodBards screens do not display its data
    yet.

## Before you start

- You must be an **administrator** or the **owner** of your GoodBards company
  account.
- You need a **Chartbeat API key**, from your Chartbeat account.
- Know the **hosts** (site addresses) Chartbeat tracks for you, for example
  `example.com`.

## How to connect

1. Open your account menu (your picture, top right), then choose **Connections**.
2. On the **Web Analytics** tab, find **Chartbeat** and choose **Connect**.
3. Fill in the form:
    - **API Key**: your Chartbeat API key.
    - **Hosts**: the site to read. Use **Add host** to add more.
4. Choose **Save Settings**. GoodBards checks each host with Chartbeat before
   saving.

To change the key or the hosts later, choose **Edit Configuration** on the card.
**Test this host** checks a saved host again.

## What GoodBards does with it

| GoodBards… | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Reads** | Real-time and historical figures for your hosts: top pages, quick stats, referrers, locations, headline tests and conversions. |
| **Does not** | Change anything in Chartbeat. |

## How to disconnect

1. On the Chartbeat card, choose **Edit Configuration**.
2. Choose **Disconnect**.
