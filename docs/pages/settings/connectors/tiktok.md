# TikTok

Publish videos to your TikTok account from a campaign.

## What it's for

The **TikTok** connector lets GoodBards post videos for you:

- **Publish and schedule TikTok videos** from a campaign's social tactic. See
  [Launch a tactic](../../marketing/launch-tactic.md) and
  [Social Post](../../social-media/social-post.md).

## Before you start

- You must be an **administrator** or the **owner** of your GoodBards company
  account.
- Have your **video ready**, in portrait (9:16) format.

## How to connect

1. Open your account menu (your picture, top right), then choose **Connections**.
2. On the **Social Media** tab, find **TikTok** and choose **Connect**.
3. Sign in to TikTok and accept the permissions it asks for: your profile and
   statistics, your videos, and uploading videos.
4. You are taken back to GoodBards. The card shows your TikTok name.

## What GoodBards does with it

| GoodBards… | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Publishes** | Your video with its caption, **visible to everyone** straight away. Duets, stitches and comments stay switched on. |
| **Reads** | Your profile and your videos' views, likes, comments and shares. |
| **Does not** | Save videos as drafts, or delete videos. |

## Good to know

- **Video only.** A TikTok post needs a video; text or pictures alone are refused.
- **2,200 characters per caption.**
- **It goes public at once.** Check the video and caption before you publish —
  there is no draft step, and GoodBards cannot remove it afterwards.
- **No manual option.** TikTok tactics do not offer the manual (publish it
  yourself) mode.
- **Access is renewed automatically.** If TikTok refuses, the connector switches
  off and you need to connect again.

## How to disconnect

1. On the TikTok card, choose **Disconnect**.
2. Confirm.

GoodBards forgets the access. Videos already published stay on TikTok.
