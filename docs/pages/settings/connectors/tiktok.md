# TikTok

Send videos and photo posts to your TikTok account from a campaign.

## What it's for

The **TikTok** connector lets GoodBards prepare TikTok posts for you:

- **Send TikTok videos and photo posts** from a campaign's social tactic, now
  or at a scheduled time. They arrive in your TikTok inbox as drafts; you
  finish and post them in the TikTok app. See
  [Launch a tactic](../../marketing/launch-tactic.md) and
  [Social Post](../../social-media/social-post.md).
- **Track the posted video** in the campaign, like any other published post.

## Before you start

- You must be an **administrator** or the **owner** of your GoodBards company
  account.
- Have your **video in the Drive** (Drive → New → Video), in portrait (9:16)
  format, or your **photos** in the Drive.

## How to connect

1. Open your account menu (your picture, top right), then choose **Connections**.
2. On the **Social Media** tab, find **TikTok** and choose **Connect**.
3. Sign in to TikTok and accept the permissions it asks for: your profile and
   statistics, your videos, sending videos to your TikTok inbox, and publishing
   videos. **Keep every box ticked.**
4. You are taken back to GoodBards. The card shows your TikTok name and picture,
   a **View profile** link, and until when the access is valid.

If you unticked a permission, the card shows **Permissions missing — reconnect**
in amber. Hover it to see which ones, then choose **Reconnect** and keep all the
boxes ticked.

## Two kinds of TikTok post

When you create the asset, choose its type:

| Type | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **TikTok Video** | One video from the Drive: MP4, MOV or WebM, up to 10 minutes and 4 GB, at least 360 pixels. The text is the caption. |
| **TikTok Photos (carousel)** | Up to 35 photos from the Drive. The main photo is the cover, the asset title is the post title (90 characters) and the text is the description (4,000 characters). |

## How a post reaches TikTok

1. In the campaign, choose **Publish** (or **Schedule**) on the TikTok asset.
2. GoodBards sends it to your **TikTok inbox as a draft**. In the campaign
   table it shows **Published** with an **In TikTok inbox** tag.
3. Open the TikTok app, open the draft from your inbox notification, finish it
   (sound, effects, who can see it) and post it.
4. Back in GoodBards, open the asset's menu and choose **Check TikTok post**.
   GoodBards links the video, and its numbers appear in the campaign report.
   If TikTok does not say which video it became (a private post), choose
   **Pick the posted video** and select it in the list.

## Posting directly (when available)

Where direct posting is switched on, **Publish** on a TikTok asset offers
**Post now on TikTok** or **Send to TikTok inbox**. Posting now shows TikTok's
own posting screen:

- **Who can view this post**: choose one; there is no default.
- **Allow users to** comment, duet or stitch: off until you tick them, and
  greyed out when they are turned off in your TikTok settings.
- **Disclose post content**: turn it on when the post promotes your own
  business (**Your brand**, labelled "Promotional content") or another brand
  (**Branded content**, labelled "Paid partnership"). Branded content cannot be
  private.
- The caption is editable, and posting means agreeing to TikTok's Music Usage
  Confirmation (and Branded Content Policy when it applies).

The post then appears on your profile after TikTok has processed it, which can
take a few minutes. In the campaign it shows **Processing on TikTok** until you
use **Check TikTok post**. Scheduled posts always go to the inbox.

## What GoodBards does with it

| GoodBards… | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Sends** | Your video, or your photos with their title and description, to your TikTok inbox as a draft. You post it yourself in the app. |
| **Reads** | Your profile and your videos' views, likes, comments and shares. |
| **Does not** | Post without you, or delete videos. |

## Good to know

- **A video draft arrives without its caption.** TikTok does not take the text
  with a video draft. In the editor, choose **Copy caption** and paste it in
  TikTok when you post. Photo posts keep their title and description.
- **5 drafts at a time.** TikTok keeps at most 5 drafts waiting in your inbox
  over 24 hours. Post or delete the waiting ones before you send more.
- **Scheduled posts arrive as drafts too.** At the scheduled time the draft
  lands in your inbox; someone still has to post it.
- **You can link a video you posted yourself.** Use **Link published post** on
  the TikTok tactic and paste the video's link
  (`tiktok.com/@your-account/video/…`) or pick it in the list.
- **Access is renewed automatically** for one year from the day you connect.
  The card shows the date; connect again before it. If TikTok refuses earlier,
  the connector switches off and you need to connect again.
- **Connect from your own session.** The TikTok sign-in must be finished within
  10 minutes, by the same person, in the same company account that started it.

## How to disconnect

1. On the TikTok card, choose **Disconnect**.
2. Confirm.

GoodBards forgets the access. Videos already posted, and drafts waiting in
your inbox, stay on TikTok.
