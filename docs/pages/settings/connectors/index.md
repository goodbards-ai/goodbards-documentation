# Connectors

Every service GoodBards can connect to, what each one gives you, and how to set
it up.

To connect any of them, open your account menu (your picture, top right), then
choose **Connections**. Only an **administrator** or the **owner** of your
company account can connect or disconnect a service. See
[Connections](../connections.md) for how the screen works.

## Social Media

| Connector | What it gives you | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LinkedIn** | Publish to your profile or company page; **@mention** people and companies; analysis and reports. | [LinkedIn](linkedin.md) |
| **Instagram** | Publish pictures, carousels, Reels and stories; account analysis and reports. | [Instagram](instagram.md) |
| **Facebook** | Publish to your Page; Page analysis and reports. | [Facebook](facebook.md) |
| **X** | Publish posts with pictures; account analysis. | [Twitter (X)](twitter-x.md) |
| **Google Youtube** | Channel analysis (read-only). | [YouTube](youtube.md) |
| **TikTok** | Send videos and photo posts to your TikTok inbox, or post them directly where available. | [TikTok](tiktok.md) |

!!! note "LinkedIn Community is now part of LinkedIn"
    Mentions used to need a second connector. The **LinkedIn** connector now
    does both. See [LinkedIn Community](linkedin-community.md).

## Good to know

- **A crown means your plan does not include it.** Speak to your account contact.
- **Disconnecting never deletes** what was already published or imported.

!!! warning "Connect with a company-owned account"
    The person who connects is the person whose access GoodBards uses. Use a
    shared, company-owned account where you can, so a colleague leaving does not
    cut your publishing off.
