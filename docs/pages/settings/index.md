# Settings

Configure your account, your company, and the services GoodBards connects to.

## In this section

- **[Personal Settings](personal-settings.md)** — your profile, language and
  notifications.
- **[Company Settings](company-settings.md)** — your company details.
- **[Brand Kit](brand-kit.md)** — logos, colours and brand voice, used wherever AI
  writes for you.
- **[Connections](connections.md)** — link the outside services you use.
- **[AI Settings](ai-settings.md)** — how AI features behave for your team.
- **[Users & Access](users-access.md)** — invite colleagues and control what they
  can do.
- **[Usage & Billing](tenant-usage.md)** — what your team has used against your
  plan.

## Setting up for the first time

In this order: [Company Settings](company-settings.md), then
[Brand Kit](brand-kit.md), then [Connections](connections.md). The brand kit
shapes everything the AI produces, so it is worth doing before generating content.
