# Settings

Configure your account, your company, your brand and the services GoodBards
connects to.

**Who sees it:** your own **Personal Settings** are open to everyone. Everything
else needs the **Settings** access.

## The screens

### Personal Settings

Your profile, password, language, default brand voice and your own AI memory.

See [Personal Settings](personal-settings.md).

### Brand & Context

What the AI knows about your company: general information, privacy statements,
your marketing campaign defaults, competitors and website.

See [Brand & Context](company-settings.md) and
[How the AI knows your brand](brand-voice-memory.md).

### Brand Kit

Logos, colours, fonts and tone of voice, used by every email, design and AI
answer.

See [Brand Kit](brand-kit.md).

### Voices Bank

Writing styles learned from real samples, such as your founder's posts.

See [How the AI knows your brand](brand-voice-memory.md).

### Connectors

Link the outside services you use: social networks, Google Analytics and Search
Console, Google Ads, your CRM.

See [Connections](connections.md).

### AI Settings

Which AI models your team uses, the tools and skills agents may use, and what the
agents have learned.

See [AI Settings](ai-settings.md).

### Users, roles & permissions

Invite colleagues and decide what each one can see and change. Access is given
per module (Home, Audience, Marketing, Emails and forms, Events, Social media,
Digital ads, Content hub, Website and SEO, AI studio, Tool box, Settings), each
in read-only or read and write. Ready-made roles cover the usual cases: a
**Marketer** sees everything, a **Sales** member sees the Audience and only the
contacts they own.

See [Users, roles & permissions](users-roles.md).

### Sub-workspaces

Separate workspaces under your own, one per client, brand or business unit.

See [Sub-Workspaces](users-access.md).

### Usage & Billing

What your team has used against your plan.

See [Usage & Billing](tenant-usage.md).

## Setting up for the first time

In this order: [Brand & Context](company-settings.md), then
[Brand Kit](brand-kit.md), then [Connections](connections.md). The brand kit
shapes everything the AI produces, so it is worth doing before generating
content.
